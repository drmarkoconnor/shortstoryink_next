# Returning students: course launch pack

The Short Story Workshop is a flexible twelve-session journey. The first two complete illustrated session packs are available in the application; sessions 3–12 are explicitly shown as in preparation. This release does not assign dates or claim the entire curriculum is ready.

## Ready to use

- `/app/course`: ethos, course map, starting point, proposed review rhythm.
- `/app/course/begin-again`: noticing, private freewriting and a first small story.
- `/app/course/someone-particular`: character, desire, contradiction and action.
- `/app/course/reading-together`: specific, humane feedback and deliberate revision.
- `/app/course/returning`: account recovery, group materials and what is saved where.
- `/app/writer/commonplace`: private notes and passages, with source, optional return link, craft tag and personal observation.

Teachers can read and print the same course pages from the Teaching studio link or account menu. Writer-only capture and drafting actions are hidden in teacher previews. Students also have a course link on their writing home and navigation. The first two lessons link directly to matching companion documents only when those documents are shared with the student's existing group. Historical database material has not been overwritten or automatically reshared.

Both session packs use original fictional examples prepared for the course, not student writing or passages attributed to Mark. The reading, exercise and revision sequence develops the principles in Mark’s existing notes. Existing artwork supplies visual continuity.

## Teaching plan to confirm with the group

Treat sessions as flexible units, not weekly deadlines. Suggested review points: a scene after session 4, a draft after session 8, a revision after session 12. Confirm dates, allowed length and teacher capacity before promising a turnaround. Other practice can remain private. Returning writers may bring an existing draft; missing a meeting should not create a compulsory backlog.

For the first meeting, introduce the course map, read “Two cups”, discuss the two noticing questions and try the five-minute exercise. Demonstrate keeping a source and personal observation in the commonplace. The modest success criterion is one detail the writer wants to follow, not a polished submission.

For the second meeting, read “The repair café” and notice where words and actions disagree. Let each writer give a character an immediate want and a complication. End by comparing two possible choices. Ask each writer to keep the scene for later work on place and viewpoint.

## Invitation draft — prepared, not sent

Welcome back to shortstory.ink.

There is now a clearer path through our reading and writing: The Short Story Workshop, from noticing to revision. We will work with short readings, small experiments and your own developing stories. You can bring a piece you already have or begin again. You do not need anything polished to return.

Sign in at https://shortstory.ink/auth/sign-in using your existing email address. If this is your first visit since the September update, choose Forgot password once to set a new password. Your earlier writing and feedback are still there.

Once inside, open Workshop course and begin with “Begin again”. There is also a private commonplace for passages and observations you want to keep. We will agree what to share and when in the group; every exercise does not need to become a submission.

If you have trouble getting in or finding our materials, contact me through our usual class channel. I look forward to reading with you again.

Mark

## Remaining live acceptance check

Before sending the invitation, verify receipt of the password-reset email for one consenting returning account and follow its newest link. Check that the account sees the intended group's handouts, can submit a small agreed test, receives published feedback and can revise. A second writer should not see that work or private commonplace. Do not use an existing student's account or reset their password without their participation.

This release's local browser tests and database privacy tests do not substitute for that inbox and authenticated production check. No invitations or reset emails were sent during implementation.
